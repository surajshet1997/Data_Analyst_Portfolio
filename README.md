## 1. End-to-End Supply Chain Analytics & Inventory Optimization (Python)

### Objective

Develop an end-to-end analytics workflow to support demand forecasting, inventory optimization, replenishment planning, supplier evaluation, and warehouse operations.

### Key Analysis

- Analyzed supply chain data at SKU–warehouse level.
- Evaluated demand patterns using exploratory data analysis.
- Compared five forecasting approaches: Naive, Seasonal Naive, Holt, Damped Holt, and Holt-Winters.
- Generated 30-day demand forecasts for 250 SKU–warehouse combinations.
- Performed ABC and XYZ inventory classification.
- Calculated safety stock and optimized reorder points.
- Developed SKU–warehouse replenishment recommendations.
- Analyzed supplier lead times and warehouse inventory allocation.
- Created structured reporting datasets for future Power BI development.

### Project Deliverables

- Demand forecasting outputs
- Inventory optimization and replenishment recommendations
- Supplier performance scorecard
- Warehouse stock-risk and inventory allocation analysis
- Power BI-ready reporting datasets
- Business insights and recommendations

## 3. Shipment Performance Analysis (SQL)

### Objective

Evaluate shipment performance, carrier reliability, route delays, and shipment revenue contribution.

### Key Insights

- Identified an on-time delivery rate of 34.36%, highlighting potential operational inefficiencies.
- Sea freight accounts for approximately 80% of shipments.
- Shanghai → Los Angeles was identified as the most delayed route.
- COSCO was identified as the most reliable carrier in the analyzed dataset.
- Europe contributes the highest shipment revenue.
